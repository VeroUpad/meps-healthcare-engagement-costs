# Healthcare Costs Among Adults with History of Substance Use

*Among adults who use tobacco or other substances, how accurately can next-year healthcare spending be predicted, and which factors drive those predictions?*

The Medical Expidenture Panel Survey is a nationally represented survey of the U.S. civilian noninstitutionalized population that follows households across multiple interview rounds to collect detailed info on health status, medical conditions, insurance, healthcare use, and spending. Since it follows families over the years, the MEPs is an excellents source to measure how one year's healthcare costs compare to the next.

We are exploring factors that can predict medical costs among adults with substance use patterns. We wanted to focus on adults who use tobacco or other substances because these populations face elevated risks of chronic disease and therefore may have higher healthcare costs.
